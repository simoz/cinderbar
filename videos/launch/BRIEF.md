## Intent

A Twitter/X launch video for Cinder Bar, the macOS port of nixfred's Burn Bar.
Same format as the agent-constellation launch video: 1440×1440, 30 fps, about
30 s, under 100 MB. Motion graphics, no narration. Story: on Omarchy, Burn Bar
in the bar; on the Mac the slot is empty; "So I ported it." and the strip
ignites in the empty slot; then what it tells you, the cockpit, the rules, and
the close with credits.
