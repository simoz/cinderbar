# Cinder Bar launch — design

Concept: a personal "port story" for a menu-bar developer tool. The original
on its home platform, the empty slot on the Mac, the drop where the port
ignites in that slot, then what it does, the depth, the rules, the close.

## Canvas and pacing

- 1440×1440, 30 fps, 30 s, for the X feed. Under 100 MB (renders ~4 MB).
- No narration. Music bed with a clear drop; the drop lands on the reveal
  line ("So I ported it.", 5.6 s). Later beats sit on the track's beat grid.
- In-feed type sizes: statements 84–132 px, captions and data ≥ 26 px.

## Palette

- Ground: charcoal #141417 with a warm radial lift; cards #222227, panel #17171b.
- Claude #D97757 · Codex #2BB5A6 · amber #FFB533 · white-hot #FFF8E6 · ember #3A1A10.
- Good #67CC73 · text #F4F1EA · muted #A19D96.
- Heat ramp: ember → agent colour → amber → white-hot (the app's own).
- The original's platform in its own theme (Omarchy: Tokyo Night #1a1b26,
  #a9b1d6, violet #7c5cff underline).

## Type

- Statements: Montserrat 900, −0.035 em, line-height 0.98.
- Chips, data, labels: JetBrains Mono 700, letter-spaced labels.
- Recreated macOS UI: Inter (stand-in for SF).

## Rules

- Rebuild the product UI in HTML at full resolution; never scale up screenshots.
- The strip keeps the same x position across bars, so the empty slot is a match cut.
- One accent per statement; credit the original author on screen twice (open and close).
- Close: icon, wordmark, mini strip, "for macOS · free · MIT", repo URL, credits and handles.
